  
  [1X16 [33X[0;0YThe Aomoto complex[133X[101X
  
  [33X[0;0YLet  [23XL[123X  be  a  geometric lattice (for instance the intersection lattice of a
  hyperplane  arrangement,  see  [2XIntersectionLattice[102X  ([14X2.2-4[114X))  with  atom set
  [23X\{1,\dots,n\}[123X      and     rank     [23Xr[123X.     Its     [13XOrlik–Solomon     algebra[113X
  [23XA^\bullet(L)=\bigoplus_{p=0}^{r}  A^p(L)[123X  is  the  quotient  of the exterior
  algebra  on  generators  [23Xe_1,\dots,e_n[123X  (one  per atom) by the Orlik–Solomon
  ideal  generated  by  the  boundaries [23X\partial e_C[123X of the circuits [23XC[123X of [23XL[123X. A
  monomial  basis  of  [23XA^p(L)[123X  is given by the [13Xno broken circuit[113X (nbc) sets of
  size [23Xp[123X.[133X
  
  [33X[0;0YGiven  a  weight  vector  [23X\lambda=(\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n)[123X  one forms the
  element [23Xa_\lambda=\sum_i \lambda_i e_i \in A^1(L)[123X. Since [23Xa_\lambda^2=0[123X, left
  multiplication by [23Xa_\lambda[123X turns [23XA^\bullet(L)[123X into a cochain complex[133X
  
  
  [24X[33X[0;6Y0        \to        A^0(L)        \xrightarrow{a_\lambda\wedge}       A^1(L)
  \xrightarrow{a_\lambda\wedge}  \cdots  \xrightarrow{a_\lambda\wedge}  A^r(L)
  \to 0,[133X
  
  [124X
  
  [33X[0;0Ythe  [13XAomoto  complex[113X  [23X(A^\bullet(L),a_\lambda\wedge-)[123X. Its cohomology is the
  combinatorial  (Aomoto)  approximation  of the cohomology of the arrangement
  complement  with  coefficients  in  the  rank  one  local  system defined by
  [23X\lambda[123X.[133X
